Privacy Policy

No Personal Data

Kivo does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

Local Images Only

All images displayed in the app are stored locally on the device. No images or data are uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server.

Image Ownership & Copyright

All images used in this application are original photos captured by the developer using a personal mobile phone. These images are fully owned by the app developer and do not include any copyrighted, licensed, or third-party content.

No Internet Access

Kivo does not require internet access and does not connect to any online services or third-party APIs.

Offline Privacy

The app works entirely offline with no tracking, analytics, or background data collection.
